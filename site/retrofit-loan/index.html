How the SBCI 3.55% Home Energy Upgrade Loan Scheme Works

The Strategic Banking Corporation of Ireland (SBCI), in partnership with the Department of the Environment and the European Investment Bank, subsidizes interest rates down to a market-low 3.55% APR for residential energy retrofits that achieve a minimum 20% energy improvement or jump to a B2/A-rating.

Because this loan is unsecured (no remortgaging or legal fees required) and repayments are stretched over 10 years, the monthly reduction in kerosene/gas/electricity bills is almost always larger than the loan payment itself—allowing homeowners to finance a full €35,000+ deep retrofit with zero upfront capital and immediate monthly profit.
