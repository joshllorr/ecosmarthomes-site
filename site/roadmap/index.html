7. 🛡️ 10 Critical Questions to Ask Installers Before Signing
1
Are you an active SEAI Registered Installer?
Confirm their company ID on the official SEAI register to guarantee grant eligibility.
2
Was the heat pump output sized using a full room-by-room heat loss calculation?
Reject generic "rules of thumb" — the unit must match the DEAP HLI output exactly.
3
What is the SCOP (Seasonal Coefficient of Performance) at 35°C flow temperature?
Look for SCOP ≥ 4.0 for maximum electricity savings.
4
Does the quotation include a high-recovery stainless steel cylinder and buffer tank?
A dedicated heat pump cylinder is required to prevent continuous hot water reheating cycles.
5
Is weather compensation included and commissioned?
Weather compensation automatically adjusts radiator temperatures based on outdoor air conditions.
6
Who is responsible for submitting the SEAI grant technical declaration?
Ensure the contractor commits to completing the final SEAI Declaration of Conformity.
7
Are electrical consumer unit (fuseboard) upgrades and surge protection included?
Avoid unexpected €800+ extras on installation day.
8
What is the manufacturer warranty on the compressor and inverter?
Look for 5 to 7-year manufacturer warranties with local Irish technical support.
9
Does the quote include chemical power flushing and a magnetic system filter (e.g. MagnaClean)?
Essential to protect the new heat pump plate heat exchanger from old radiator sludge.
10
Are there any hidden subcontracting or commissioning charges?
Ensure the quotation is 100% fixed-price and turnkey.
