How the Clean Export Guarantee (CEG) Works in Ireland

Under the Irish Clean Export Guarantee (CEG), electricity suppliers (such as Electric Ireland, Bord Gáis, Energia, and SSE Airtricity) are legally mandated to pay domestic solar microgenerators for every kilowatt-hour (kWh) exported back to the ESB Networks national grid. Rates typically range between 21c and 24c per kWh, credited directly to your utility bill as cash earnings.

Combined with the 0% VAT government incentive and up to €1,800 in SEAI Solar PV grants, modern rooftop solar in Ireland delivers an unprecedented average return on investment of 22% annually.
