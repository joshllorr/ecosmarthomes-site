# Complete Home Energy Retrofit Roadmap

A comprehensive, step-by-step master plan for retrofitting your home in Ireland to modern NZEB (Nearly Zero Energy Building) standards.

---

## 1. Initial Assessment & Energy Audit
Before committing to any contractor quotes:
- Engage an independent SEAI registered assessor.
- Conduct a blower door airtightness test and thermal imaging scan.
- Receive a detailed Home Energy Assessment report detailing heat loss indicator (HLI).

---

## 2. Fabric-First Upgrades
Focus on reducing heat demand before sizing heating systems:
- Attic & Ceiling Insulation (300mm cross-laid).
- Wall Insulation (External Wall Insulation or Cavity Injection).
- Floor & Perimeter Insulation.
- Windows & High Performance A-Rated Doors.

---

## 3. Renewable Heating & Solar Systems
- **Air-to-Water Heat Pump**: High-efficiency heating operating at 35°C–45°C flow temperature.
- **Solar PV Micro-Generation**: 4kWp+ rooftop array to generate clean electricity.
- **Smart Energy Controls**: Zone-based heating controls with weather compensation.

---

## 4. SEAI Grant Funding Breakdown

- **Attic Insulation**: Up to €1,500
- **Cavity Wall Insulation**: Up to €1,700
- **External Wall Insulation**: Up to €8,000
- **Heat Pump System**: Up to €6,500
- **Solar PV System**: Up to €2,100
- **Total Combined Grant Value**: Up to €19,800+
