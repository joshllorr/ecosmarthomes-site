BER Assessment Analysis

Your BER is more than a letter — it’s the clearest snapshot of how your home is performing today. We translate your BER into plain English so you understand what’s working, what’s wasting energy, and where your money will make the biggest impact.

  • Where heat is being lost in your home
  • Clear upgrade priorities based on comfort and cost
  • Independent advice with no sales agenda
  • Expected improvements from each upgrade
  • A realistic path to raising your BER rating

SEAI Grant Navigation

The SEAI grant system can feel like a maze. We simplify it by showing you exactly what funding you qualify for and how to structure your upgrades to maximise support.

  • All grants available for your home type
  • How much funding you can realistically expect
  • How to combine grants without breaking SEAI rules
  • Best timing for applications
  • A clear, step‑by‑step plan tailored to your home

Retrofit Planning

A successful retrofit isn’t about doing everything at once — it’s about doing the right upgrades in the right order. We build a personalised roadmap that protects your budget and maximises long‑term comfort.

  • A clear sequence of recommended upgrades
  • Estimated costs and realistic savings
  • Grant opportunities at each stage
  • Impact of each upgrade on comfort and BER
  • A timeline that fits your budget and lifestyle

Ventilation Strategy

Good insulation needs good ventilation. Without it, moisture builds up, air quality drops, and your energy savings disappear. We design a ventilation approach that keeps your home healthy, dry, and efficient.

  • How air currently moves through your home
  • Moisture risks and condensation hotspots
  • Options for natural, demand‑controlled, or MVHR systems
  • How ventilation supports airtightness
  • Solutions that improve comfort without wasting heat
