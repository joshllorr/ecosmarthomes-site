# Raising BER from G to A: Step-by-Step Retrofit Sequence

Upgrading a G-rated home to an A-rating requires a structured, sequence-driven strategy. Skipping steps or installing a heat pump into an uninsulated building will lead to high electricity bills and poor comfort.

---

## The 5-Step Retrofit Roadmap

### Step 1: Roof & Attic Insulation (G ➔ E Jump)
- **Target**: 300mm cross-laid mineral wool insulation.
- **Cost**: ~€1,800
- **SEAI Grant**: €1,500
- **Net Cost**: €300

### Step 2: Wall Insulation (E ➔ D Jump)
- **Target**: External Wall Insulation (EWI 100mm-120mm EPS) for solid walls or bonded bead cavity injection for cavity walls.
- **SEAI Grant**: €1,700 (Cavity) / €6,000 - €8,000 (EWI).

### Step 3: Windows & Air Tightness (D ➔ C Jump)
- **Target**: Triple glazing (U-value ≤ 0.8 W/m²K) and draught sealing around window boards and floor joints.

### Step 4: Heat Pump Integration (C ➔ B1 Jump)
- **Target**: Air-to-Water heat pump system with weather compensation controls.
- **SEAI Grant**: €6,500 + €200 technical assessment grant.

### Step 5: Solar PV Micro-Generation (B1 ➔ A2 Jump)
- **Target**: 4.5 kWp Solar PV array + 5 kWh battery storage.
- **SEAI Grant**: €2,100
- **Result**: BER A2 NZEB Rating achieved.
