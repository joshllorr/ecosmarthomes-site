Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about our pricing, travel, and independence.

🛡️ Why are you 100% independent? Do you sell heat pumps or insulation?

We do NOT sell hardware, install equipment, or accept commissions from retrofit companies. When an installer visits your house for a "free quote", their incentive is to sell you the most expensive unit they stock. Our incentive is 100% aligned with yours: giving you conflict-free engineering calculations so you get the exact right size, avoid overpaying, and maximize SEAI grants.

📍 Do you travel to all 32 counties in Ireland?

Yes! We regularly conduct on-site engineering surveys across Dublin, Cork, Limerick, Galway, Waterford, Kerry, Kildare, Wicklow, and all other Irish counties. Travel across Ireland is included in our flat on-site fees.

💶 What is the difference between the €49 pack and the €149 survey?

The €49 Installer Pack is a digital-only deliverable: you upload photos and room dimensions, and we return calculated radiator outputs and heat loss within 24–48 hours. The €149 Home Energy Diagnostic Survey is a comprehensive, in-person on-site visit by Joe, where we perform physical thermal scans, inspect your attic and walls, check electrical supply, and create your complete 12-page SEAI grant roadmap.

🤝 How does the 100% Money-Back Guarantee work?

If our on-site survey and quote audit do not identify at least €500 in contractor savings or grant optimizations for your home retrofit, simply let us know and we will refund your survey fee in full. No hassle.
