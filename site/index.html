About EcoSmart Home

EcoSmart Home was built on a simple idea: homeowners deserve clear, honest guidance without the sales pressure. I'm not an installer, I don't earn commissions, and I don't benefit from pushing heat pumps, solar panels, or any specific upgrade. My only job is to help you understand your home and make smarter decisions that protect your comfort, your wallet, and your future.

Most people feel lost when it comes to energy upgrades. Installers want to sell hardware. Low‑cost assessors rush paperwork. Technical experts drown you in jargon. I take a different approach — the neighbourly one.

I explain your home in plain English: where the heat is leaking, whether your radiators can support a modern system, and what steps will actually make a difference. You get a Retrofit Roadmap™ that shows the smartest upgrades, the real costs, the grants you can claim, and how to build your Carbon Tax Shield to stay ahead of rising energy prices and future government levies.

Heat Pump Test

A heat pump can be a brilliant upgrade — but only if your home is ready for it. Most homes fail the basic requirements, which leads to oversized systems, high running costs, and poor performance. The Heat Pump Test checks your home's readiness before you spend thousands.

You'll learn:

  • Whether your home can actually support a heat pump
  • What upgrades (if any) are needed to pass the test
  • How to avoid oversizing, poor installs, and wasted money
  • The most cost‑effective path to becoming heat‑pump ready

This is independent, conflict‑free guidance. No sales, no commissions — just a clear answer before you make a big investment.

Carbon Tax Shield

Energy prices and government levies are rising every year, and most homeowners have no idea how exposed their home really is. The Carbon Tax Shield shows you the financial impact of your current setup and what it will cost you over the next decade if nothing changes.

You'll see:

  • Where your home is losing money today
  • How future carbon taxes will affect your bills
  • The upgrades that protect you from rising levies
  • The smartest order to complete them for maximum savings

This isn't about "being greener." It's about protecting your wallet and future-proofing your home with clear, independent advice.

Renovation Feasibility Study

Most homeowners start upgrades without knowing what's realistic, what's risky, or what will actually improve comfort and reduce costs. The Renovation Feasibility Study gives you a clear, independent overview of your home before you spend a cent on works or contractors.

You'll learn:

  • What upgrades make sense for your specific home
  • What risks or hidden issues could cost you later
  • Which improvements deliver the best comfort and savings
  • Whether your plans are achievable within your budget

This is not a sales call and not a quote. It's a conflict‑free assessment that helps you avoid expensive mistakes and plan with confidence.

Leakiness Score

Most homes lose far more heat than homeowners realise. Warm air escapes through tiny gaps, drafts sneak in around old fittings, and insulation often performs worse than expected. The Leakiness Score shows you exactly where your home is leaking money — explained in plain English.

You'll learn:

  • Where warm air is escaping and cold air is getting in
  • How much these leaks are costing you every year
  • Which fixes give the biggest comfort boost for the lowest cost
  • What to prioritise before investing in bigger upgrades

No engineering jargon. No pressure to buy anything. Just a clear, neighbourly explanation of how to keep more heat — and more money — inside your home.

Cold Spots & Mould Risk Check

Cold patches on walls and ceilings are more than just uncomfortable — they're early warning signs of damp and mould. Most homeowners don't notice them until the problem becomes expensive. This check highlights the areas at risk and explains what's causing them in simple, practical terms.

You'll learn:

  • Where cold spots are forming and why they happen
  • How close your home is to developing damp or mould
  • What small fixes can prevent bigger problems later
  • Which upgrades improve comfort and protect your home's structure

No scare tactics. No upselling. Just clear, independent guidance to keep your home healthy, warm, and worry‑free.

Grant Eligibility & Readiness Audit

Most homeowners miss out on grant funding because key details are rushed, overlooked, or completed by someone whose priority is selling hardware. This audit ensures your home qualifies for the maximum grants available — and that nothing causes you to lose money later.

You'll learn:

  • Which grants your home can access right now
  • What upgrades are required to unlock higher funding
  • Common mistakes that lead to rejected or reduced grant payments
  • How to sequence your upgrades to maximise financial support

This is not "grant paperwork." It's a strategic financial review that protects your funding and keeps every euro working in your favour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free consultations?

No. I'm a consultant, not a salesperson. I offer a short 15‑minute Suitability Check to confirm your home and goals are a good fit for an Independent Retrofit Assessment. All expert guidance is provided within paid services only.

What is the Heat Pump Test?

It's a simple pass/fail check to confirm whether your home is ready for a heat pump before you spend thousands. If it won't pass, I show you the exact steps to fix it.

What is the Leakiness Score?

Instead of engineering jargon, I show you where warm air escapes and cold air sneaks in — explained in plain English. Think of your home like a bucket: if it's leaky, you'll never keep the heat in.

Grant Eligibility & Readiness Audit

Ensure your home qualifies for maximum grants and avoid costly mistakes that cause lost funding.

Learn More →

What is the Retrofit Roadmap™?

It's your personalised upgrade plan showing what to do, when to do it, what it costs, what grants you can claim, and the order that saves you the most money. It's the opposite of a BER certificate — it's a long‑term strategy for your home.

What is the Carbon Tax Shield?

It's a financial‑defence plan that shows how today's upgrades protect you from rising energy costs and future government levies. This isn't about "being greener" — it's about protecting your wallet.

Do you work with any installers?

No. I stay fully independent so you get unbiased advice. If you need installers later, I help you understand what to ask and what to avoid — but I don't take commissions or referrals.

Can you check if my radiators are suitable for a heat pump?

Yes. This is part of the Radiator Check. I explain in plain English whether your current radiators can support a modern system and what changes (if any) are needed.

How long does the assessment take?

Most assessments take between 60 and 90 minutes, depending on the size and condition of the home. You'll receive your Retrofit Roadmap™ shortly after.

Is this service only for older homes?

No. Even newer homes can be leaky, inefficient, or poorly set up for future heating systems. Every home benefits from a clear, independent plan.
