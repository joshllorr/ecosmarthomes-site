How do SEAI grants work for Heat Pumps & Insulation?

The SEAI provides up to €12,500 for heat pumps, €8,000 for external wall wrap, €2,500 for attic insulation, and €1,800 for solar PV. Grants are paid directly to your registered installer and deducted upfront from your invoice.

How does the new May 2026 simplified BER scale affect my home?

On May 24, 2026, SEAI consolidated the 15-band sub-divisions into 8 clear categories (A0, A, B, C, D, E, F, G). Achieving a 'B' rating qualifies you for 3.45% Green Mortgage interest rates, and an 'A' or 'A0' rating achieves Ireland's official NZEB zero-emission standard.
