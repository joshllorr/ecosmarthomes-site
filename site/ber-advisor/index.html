Fabric-First Upgrade Strategy

🧱

1. Fabric-First Insulation

Seal thermal leaks before upgrading heating equipment. Attic insulation and cavity wall insulation deliver the highest return per euro spent.

Step 1 • Immediate Payback
🔥

2. Heat Pump & Controls

Replace old oil or gas boilers with an Air-to-Water Heat Pump once your home passes the HLI heat loss test (< 2.0 W/K·m²).

Step 2 • Up to €12,500 Heat Pump Grant
☀️

3. Solar PV Microgeneration

Generate clean electricity on-site to offset day-rate power costs and export surplus back to the grid under the Clean Export Guarantee.

Step 3 • Up to €1,800 Solar PV Grant
💶
