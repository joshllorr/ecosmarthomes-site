Raising BER from G to A: Step-by-Step Retrofit Sequence

Independent guidance for Irish homeowners

Key Takeaways (SEAI May 2026 & EPBD 2030 Standards)

  • Order matters more than budget: Fabric first (external wrap, attic 300mm), then heat pump, then rooftop solar PV.
  • G & F homes qualify for the largest grants: Up to €25,500+ through SEAI One-Stop-Shop & deep retrofit supports.
  • EPBD 2030 EU Mandates: Worst-performing residential dwellings (G & F) face mandatory efficiency benchmarks across the EU.
  • The Heat Pump Readiness Rule: NSAI SR50-2 requires a Heat Loss Index (HLI) ≤ 2.0 W/K·m² before heat pump grant sign-off.
  • Property Value Uplift: Documented +12% to +16% increase in Irish home market value when leaping from G to A2.

If your BER cert has a G on it, you're not alone, and you're not stuck. Approximately 10% of Irish dwellings still sit in the F or G band. Under the May 2026 SEAI framework, homes starting at G or F unlock the highest level of government funding (€25,500+) and can reduce annual heating bills from ~€5,400 down to ~€550 per year.

🚀 The Official G ➔ A / A0 Engineering Pathway

How each stage transforms your primary energy use and annual fuel expense under the revised EPBD framework:

🔴 G Rating (Starting Point):
>375 kWh/m²/yr · ~€5,400/yr fuel · 8.5 t CO₂
🟢 B Rating (SEAI Base Standard):
≤150 kWh/m²/yr · ~€1,200/yr fuel · 1.6 t CO₂
🟢 A & A0 Rating (Deep Retrofit & ZEB Target):
≤75 kWh (A) down to ≤42 kWh/m²/yr (A0) · ~€350–€650/yr fuel · 0.2–0.6 t CO₂ · +16% Property Value

Step 1: Get an Independent Assessment Before You Begin

You can't fix what you haven't measured. A proper independent survey gives you your Heat Loss Index (HLI) score, radiator sizing Delta T check, and an anti-scam installer scope of works.

Step 2: Fix the Fabric First (External Wall Wrap & Attic)

External wall insulation (U-value ≤ 0.18 W/m²K) and 300mm attic mineral wool top-up deliver the highest energy reduction per euro spent, qualifying for €10,500 in combined grants.

Step 3: The Heat Pump Test (HLI ≤ 2.0 & SR50 Compliance)

Under NSAI SR50-2 and SR54, all open fireplaces must be permanently sealed or converted to room-sealed stoves, and heat loss must drop below 2.0 W/K·m².

Step 4: Add Solar PV & Microgeneration (0% VAT)

A 4.3 kWp (10-panel) all-black monocrystalline system produces ~4,100 kWh/year, qualifying for €1,800 in SEAI grants and generating ~€385/year in Clean Export Guarantee (CEG) income.
