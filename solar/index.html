How the Clean Export Guarantee (CEG) & 0% VAT Work in Ireland

Under the Irish Clean Export Guarantee (CEG), electricity suppliers (Electric Ireland, Bord Gáis, Energia, SSE Airtricity, Yuno) are legally mandated to pay domestic solar microgenerators for every kilowatt-hour (kWh) exported back to the ESB Networks national grid. Rates typically range between 21c and 24c per kWh, credited directly to your utility bill as cash earnings.

Combined with the government's 0% VAT incentive on domestic solar PV installations and up to €1,800 in SEAI Solar PV grants, modern rooftop solar in Ireland delivers an unprecedented average return on investment of 22% annually.
