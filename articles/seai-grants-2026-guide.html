The Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI) provides non-repayable capital grants to help Irish homeowners transition away from fossil heating, eradicate draughts, and achieve an A2 Building Energy Rating (BER). Here is the complete official rate schedule for individual energy measures and whole-home One-Stop-Shop upgrades.

How to Avoid Grant Rejection

The most common reason SEAI grant applications are delayed or rejected is uncertified contractor installation or failure to submit a post-works BER assessment. Always ensure your installer is registered on the active SEAI register and that your initial fabric assessment is engineered independently.
